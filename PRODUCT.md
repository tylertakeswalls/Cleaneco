# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
Both clients booking waste management services and admins managing operations and processing orders.

## Product Purpose
A dashboard and client portal for Cleaneco Waste Management Services to manage declogging, deep cleaning, and order processing workflows. Success looks like a smooth, reliable booking experience for clients and an efficient management interface for admins.

## Positioning
Reliable, professional waste management made easy.

## Brand Personality
Clean, Professional, Trustworthy (like modern SaaS tools)

## Anti-references
Avoid outdated, clunky 90s enterprise portals.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over cleverness**: Keep navigation and actions unambiguous.
- **Expert confidence**: The design should assure users that their waste management needs are handled professionally.
- **Workflow efficiency**: Admins should be able to process orders and reports with minimal friction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard best practices.
